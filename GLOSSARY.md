## Detector

#### Time Gate Width 
The duration of the time interval during which the detector is active and capable of registering incoming photons.

It determines the temporal resolution of the photon counting system, affecting how precisely the arrival times of photons can be recorded.

Shorter time gate widths provide higher temporal resolution but may reduce detection efficiency as they may miss photons arriving slightly outside the gate window.

#### Dead Time
The period of time immediately following the detection of a photon during which the detector is unable to register subsequent photons.

#### Repetition Time
The number of photon pulses arriving at the detector per unit time. It indicates how frequently the detector is exposed to photon pulses.
